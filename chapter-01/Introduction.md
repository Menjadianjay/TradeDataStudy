# Description

International Trade is a kind of trade done by two sides of different regions (a person, group, or goverment)
to establish different goal based on agreed terms.

# Pros

There's some pros that can be achieve of International Trade :

1. International Trade can open job opportunity and reduce unemployed rate.
2. Spread market range and escalate income due sold goods to different regions.
3. Establish international relationship that opens many posibilities.
4. Escalate country prosperity.

# Cons

There's some cons that can prevent International Trade between countries to occcur.

1. Different currency can be an issue due to different currency exchange rate.
2. Trade Policies of each country due to some county can have import restriction.
3. Conflict that include the country.
4. Export and import policies.
5. Low Quality of Human Resources.

# Types

There's some example of International Trade.

1. Export-Import.
2. Barter.
3. Consignment.
4. Border Crossing.
5. Package Deal.
